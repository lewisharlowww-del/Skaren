# Skaren — Influencer Outreach Kit (Norway)

Goal: INSTALLS. Target micro-influencers (5k–50k followers). They convert
better per krone than big names and often post for free Premium + a small fee.

---

## 1) WHO TO TARGET

Search these hashtags on Instagram + TikTok and note accounts with 5k–50k
followers and good engagement (lots of comments, not just likes):

- #sunnmat  #kosthold  #ernæring  #sunnhverdag  #matglede
- #tilsetningsstoffer  #renmat  #utentilsetning
- #lavkarbo  #keto  #sukkerfri
- #gravid  #babymat  #småbarnsmat   (parents care most about additives)
- #norskmat  #matbudsjett  #handletips

Creator types that fit best:
- Nutritionists / ernæringsfysiologer (credibility)
- Clean-eating / additive-aware home cooks
- Parents doing healthy family food
- Fitness / lavkarbo label-readers

Starter accounts found (verify follower counts before contacting):
- @desiree_sunnlivsstil  (TikTok, sunn mat)
- @sunnhjernemat         (TikTok, klinisk ernæringsfysiolog)
- @nutritac.no           (nutritionist, ingredients)
- Anette Skarpaas Ramm   (klinisk ernæringsfysiolog, media)
- Tanja Kalchenko        (lege, sunt kosthold)

---

## 2) THE OFFER (keep it simple)

Tier 1 (nano, <10k):  Free lifetime Premium  ->  1 Reel/TikTok + 1 Story
Tier 2 (10k–50k):     Free Premium + 300–800 NOK  ->  1 video + 2 stories w/ link
Bonus for all:        Unique promo/referral code (tracks installs)

Always give them a unique link or code so you can measure installs per creator.

Si ifra om du vil ha en gratis tilgang, så sender jeg med en gang! 🙌

## 4) DM TEMPLATE — ENGLISH (backup)

Hi [Name]! I built Skaren, a Norwegian app that scans food barcodes and shows
health + eco grades and which additives (E-numbers) a product contains.

Your content on [topic] fits perfectly with what we help people do. Would you
like free Premium access to try it, and make a short video if you like it?
You'd get your own code for your followers. Happy to send access right away!

---

## 5) OUTREACH RULES

- DM 10–15 creators/day, personalize line 3 to a REAL post of theirs.
- Follow + like 2 posts BEFORE the DM (warms them up).
- No link in first DM (Instagram throttles it). Send link after they reply yes.
- Follow up ONCE after 3–4 days if no reply.
- Track everything in influencer-outreach-tracker.csv (in this folder).

---

## 6) WHAT TO MEASURE

- Installs per creator (via unique code/link)
- Cost per install (fee / installs)
- Double down on creators under ~15 NOK/install; drop the rest.
