## WHY IT'S WRITTEN THIS WAY (notes for you)

- **Line 2 personalization** ([specific post]) is the single most important part.
  Replace it with a REAL post of theirs, or they'll treat it as spam.
- **You ask for price AND scope in one go** so they can't reply with just a number
  and leave you guessing. This saves 2-3 back-and-forth messages.
- **"Small independent creator"** sets expectations low and invites a fair rate.
- **No link in the first message** on Instagram (it gets throttled/hidden). Add the
  link only after they reply. On email you can include it.
- **Asking about ad reuse** matters: if you can rerun their video as a paid ad,
  that's often worth more than the organic post itself.

## AFTER THEY REPLY WITH A PRICE

- If the fee ÷ their followers feels high, counter: "Could we do free Premium +
  [about half] + your code, and I'll also feature you in the app's shoutout?"
- Always insist on the **unique code/link** so you can measure installs.
- Rule of thumb: if it costs more than ~15 NOK per install, don't repeat it.
